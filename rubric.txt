STEP 3 — RUBRIC
---------------
Scoring scale for every criterion: 0–5
  5 = strong, specific evidence (numbers, named artefact, adoption)
  3 = some evidence, partial or generic
  1 = claimed but unsupported
  0 = no evidence
Weighted score = sum(score / 5 × weight). Maximum 100 per role.

[PM] Product Manager — core operations platform (2–4 yrs)
PM1 | Ground-floor operations exposure | 25 | Has done operational work in logistics, freight, supply chain or another ops-heavy floor (not just interviewed users). Backed by P1: Rohan, Sunita, Meghna, Lavanya, Aditya.
PM2 | Unprompted builder with adoption | 25 | Built a tool, process or feature without being asked, and others adopted it; adoption numbers count most. Backed by P2: Rohan's tracker, Sunita's redesign, Lavanya's dashboard, Meghna's framework.
PM3 | Ship, kill and learn in short cycles | 20 | Evidence of shipping to real users, killing what didn't work, and writing down why (post-mortems, pivots driven by usage data). Backed by P4: Lavanya, Aditya.
PM4 | Ownership without scaffolding | 20 | Owned a product area or outcome alone, built the missing process/rhythm themselves, no senior layer making the calls. Backed by P3: Lavanya (sole PM), Rohan, Meghna.
PM5 | Stage and level fit | 10 | Roughly 2–4 years of product (or equivalent building) experience at an early or growth-stage company; Mumbai/relocation-ready. Low-rated Vikram shows big-platform polish is not the target.
